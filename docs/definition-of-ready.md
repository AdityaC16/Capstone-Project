# Definition of Ready

**DRAFT. Team to agree on Day 1.** A story can be pulled into a sprint when:

- [ ] It's written as a user story with clear customer or team value
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written and testable
- [ ] It's small enough to finish in ≤ 1 day (otherwise split it)
- [ ] Dependencies (API contract, schema, other stories) are known and available
- [ ] Security/data considerations are noted (input, data stored, secrets)
- [ ] It has an owner and is on the board
